How to read this

Base unit
The unit stock is counted in. Rod is held in KG, so twenty kilos can be issued even though it was bought by the ton. স্টক এই এককেই গোনা হয়।
Also entered as
Other units the operator may type on a challan. The software converts them itself - nobody does the arithmetic. চালানে যে একক লেখা, সেই এককেই এন্ট্রি দেবেন।
adjustable
A unit whose real size varies from delivery to delivery - a truck of sand is not the same hundred cubic feet every trip. The operator may correct the conversion on the day. মাপ প্রতিবার সমান হয় না, তাই এন্ট্রিতে বদলানো যায়।

Once stock has moved against an item, its base unit can no longer be changed. It is worth a minute at setup to check the units suit the way this company buys.
